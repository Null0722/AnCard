# Issuer-source research: initial 17-card seed

**Research checked:** 2026-10-29 (U.S. market). **Editorial status:** pending human QC on every record below. This is a dated research packet, not approval, an application offer, or a guarantee of current rates.

## Guardrails

- Human approval stays false in `catalog/HUMAN-QC.json` until an authorized reviewer cross-checks the individual issuer's pricing, reward agreement, limitations, eligibility and promotional conditions. An automated source lookup does not satisfy AnCard's human QC gate.
- Prefer issuer product, rewards and agreement pages. An issuer category listing is useful corroboration, not a substitute for the specific card's full terms.
- Only select stable, clearly sourced fee and earning claims for the review candidate. Do not use varying or channel-targeted welcome offers as universally available. Check APRs, caps, merchant-coding exceptions, annual-fee timing, bonus history, transfer terms and point redemption rules immediately before final human approval.
- No extra images were licensed or made public. No production card facts were published as a result of this research.
