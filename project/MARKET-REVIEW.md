# Consumer market review: what people want to know about credit cards

*Research snapshot: October 27, 2026. Product design analysis, not a card ranking or individualized financial recommendation.*

## Sources used

1. [J.D. Power 2025 U.S. Credit Card Satisfaction Study](https://www.jdpower.com/business/press-releases/2025-us-credit-card-satisfaction-study). Explores account management, benefits, new accounts, rewards earning, redemption, service and terms. It reported 53% of surveyed cardholders carrying revolving debt.
2. [CFPB 2025 Consumer Credit Card Market Report](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/data-research/research-reports/the-consumer-credit-card-market-2025/). Examines promotional interest rates, spending by merchant category, card availability, costs and consumer disputes.
3. [CFPB 2024 Rewards Program Circular](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/compliance/circulars/consumer-financial-protection-circular-2024-07-design-marketing-and-administration-of-credit-card-rewards-programs/). Identifies complaints about hidden sign-up restrictions, revoked rewards, unpredictable redemption and devaluation.
4. [Consumer.gov Credit Card Comparison Guidance](https://consumer.gov/credit/getting-credit-card). Encourages examining APR, annual fee and other fees, not only rewards.
5. [CFPB Credit Limit Explanation](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/ask-cfpb/why-did-i-get-a-low-credit-limit-on-a-credit-card-en-11/) and [Regulation Z Ability to Pay](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/rules-policy/regulations/1026/51/). Establish the roles of credit history, income/assets and current obligations in limits and approvals.
6. [CFPB Prescreened Credit Offers](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/ask-cfpb/what-is-a-prescreened-credit-card-offer-en-1/). Prescreening does not guarantee approval and terms can vary.
7. Current first-party issuer material: [Chase Freedom Unlimited](https://creditcards.chase.com/cash-back-credit-cards/freedom/unlimited), [Chase Freedom Flex](https://creditcards.chase.com/cash-back-credit-cards/freedom/flex), [Capital One Venture X](https://www.capitalone.com/credit-cards/venture-x/), [Amex credit card catalog](https://www.americanexpress.com/us/credit-cards/) and [Amex Membership Rewards point valuation examples](https://www.americanexpress.com/en-us/credit-cards/credit-intel/american-express-points-value/).

## Discoverability priorities

| Consumer concern | What AnCard explains | Editorial safeguard |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What will I pay? | Annual/monthly fees, intro and variable APR, balance transfer/foreign fees, penalty triggers, grace periods | Never interpret absent data as $0 |
| What can I earn? | Cash back vs points/miles; earn multipliers; rotating/choice categories; merchant coding and caps | Multipliers do not equal dollar redemption values |
| What is my welcome bonus? | Amount or "as high as" qualifier, eligible spending requirement, deadline, excluded transactions, prior-bonus restrictions | Offer is dated and channel-dependent; not guaranteed |
| Could I get a better targeted offer? | Distinguish public, personally targeted, prescreened, and true invitation-only offers | Do not manufacture a targeted offer or suggest eligibility |
| What is one point worth? | Evidence-backed cents-per-point examples by redemption method; transfer rates; expiration | Avoid unsourced universal point valuation |
| Will I be approved? | Published issuer credit-level descriptions if available; obligations and history; income/asset ability to pay | No fabricated "typical score", approval probability or minimum score |
| How high is my opening credit limit? | Issuer-stated minimum, if one exists; otherwise underwriting explanation | A high credit score cannot promise a limit |
| Which benefits are usable? | Credits and recurrence, Global Entry/TSA PreCheck, lounge access, rental/trip/phone protection | Qualifying purchases, enrollment, caps, exclusions and reimbursement timing matter |
| How do I manage risk? | Payment behavior, lost grace period, credit inquiries, variable APR, cash advance rules, account security | No account connection or user financial data collection |

## Design implementation

Decode Terms uses **progressive disclosure**, with clear subject headings and expandable sections. General education remains visible even if card-specific proposed figures have not been human-verified. Once approved, issuer-backed fields appear in the same sections, clearly distinguished from general advice.

Side-by-side comparison repeats the same categories with equal emphasis, not a card score. Any unverified number is marked pending or not documented. The public directory remains alphabetical and filter-first. AnCard does not store scores, income, browsing filters or spending habits.

### Special care for credit scores

A typical approved FICO score cannot be inferred from anecdotes, a marketing "Excellent" label or the expected card tier. AnCard should use numerical score distributions only if a documented source describes actual approval outcomes with a clear methodology, population and timeframe. Currently, no such card-specific distribution has been established. Score is one input, not the deciding or sole input; income, debts, assets, credit history and issuer policy matter.

### Source volatility

Intro offers, APRs, credit terms, eligibility and point redemption rates may change independently. Each proposed amount needs exact source, review date, restrictions, and a new human approval if edited. All research remains subject to the existing `humanVerified` publishing gate.
