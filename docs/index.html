THE PLAIN-ENGLISH DECODER

Small print, made readable.

Examples here are general explanations, not issuer-specific terms.

01

Purchase APR

The interest rate that may apply when you carry an eligible purchase balance. Some rates vary with an index, and promotional rates can expire.

Look for: variable rates, grace-period rules and promotional end dates.

02

Reward categories

A card may reward grocery, dining or travel spending differently. Merchant coding, caps and activation rules can change what counts.

Look for: exclusions, spending limits and redemption restrictions.

03

Penalty APR

Certain missed payments can trigger a higher interest rate under the issuer's terms. When and how it applies varies by agreement.

Look for: trigger events, notice rules and how long a penalty rate lasts.

04

Welcome bonuses

Public bonuses may require qualifying purchases by a deadline. Personalized offers and invitation-only cards are separate concepts.

Look for: Eligible spending, account history, offer channel and expiration.

05

Targeted and invite-only offers

An invitation or prescreened offer is not guaranteed approval or a specific credit line.

Look for: Written conditions, eligibility and the exact offered amount.

06

Starting credit limits

Most starting limits are set after reviewing a credit application. No universal typical opening limit is reliable.

Look for: Income, obligations, credit history and any issuer-disclosed minimum.

07

Credit-score guidance

A score matters, but no universal score cutoff guarantees approval. Debt, income, assets and credit history also count.

Look for: Evidence from the issuer, not anecdotes presented as approval odds.

08

Cents per point

A 5X multiplier is not automatically 5% cash back. Redemption choices determine the money value.

Look for: Cash-back redemption, travel portal rules and transfer partner rates.

09

Rotating versus selectable categories

Bonus categories may rotate quarterly or require the cardholder to select them.

Look for: Enrollment, spending caps, eligible merchant codes and rates after the cap.

10

Balance transfers and foreign fees

Introductory APRs can end, balance transfers can carry fees, and foreign fees can vary.

Look for: Regular APR, transfer window, promotional expiry and grace period.

11

Travel credits and protections

Global Entry, lounge access and insurance can be valuable but are conditional.

Look for: Reimbursement amount, frequency, enrollment and exclusions.

12

Rewards redemption restrictions

Rewards can be forfeited or lose redemption value under program rules.

Look for: Cash-out ratios, card closure policies, expiry and redemption minimums.

THE WAY WE WORK

Verified, not guessed.

Our catalog is designed to favor clear documentation over a long list of offers. The catalog uses a human verification gate: proposed terms are hidden until manually confirmed; changing card details revokes approval until reviewed again.

01 / Sources

Issuer pages and card agreements take priority over marketing summaries. Every real listing will link to its source.

02 / Ordering

Alphabetical and factual sort options only. Filters use your selections; no hidden suitability score.

03 / Corrections

Outdated or disputed listings can be withdrawn without erasing the internal change history.

04 / Funding

No card referral commissions. Future contextual ads or donations must be disclosed and must not influence listings.

Privacy pledge

AnCard does not ask for an account or collect your card-filter choices. Filtering and comparisons run in your browser memory; we do not use analytics, cookies or third-party advertising scripts in this MVP. GitHub Pages, our hosting provider, records visitor IP addresses for security purposes. We cannot claim that visiting the site creates no network logs.

Important notices

This website is educational, not personalized financial, credit, legal or tax advice. Card terms change, and issuer agreements govern. Eligibility and approval decisions belong to issuers. Unreviewed records display placeholders. Even after review, the issuer agreement governs fees, APRs and rewards. No application or affiliate links are included. Always verify real card information directly with the issuer before acting.
