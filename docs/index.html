THE PLAIN-ENGLISH DECODER

Small print, made readable.

Examples here are general explanations, not issuer-specific terms.

01

Purchase APR

The interest rate that may apply when you carry an eligible purchase balance. Some rates vary with an index, and promotional rates can expire.

Look for: variable rates, grace-period rules and promotional end dates.

02

Reward categories

A card may reward grocery, dining or travel spending differently. Merchant coding, caps and activation rules can change what counts.

Look for: exclusions, spending limits and redemption restrictions.

03

Penalty APR

Certain missed payments can trigger a higher interest rate under the issuer's terms. When and how it applies varies by agreement.

Look for: trigger events, notice rules and how long a penalty rate lasts.

THE WAY WE WORK

Verified, not guessed.

Our catalog is designed to favor clear documentation over a long list of offers. The catalog uses a human verification gate: proposed terms are hidden until manually confirmed; changing card details revokes approval until reviewed again.

01 / Sources

Issuer pages and card agreements take priority over marketing summaries. Every real listing will link to its source.

02 / Ordering

Alphabetical and factual sort options only. Filters use your selections; no hidden suitability score.

03 / Corrections

Outdated or disputed listings can be withdrawn without erasing the internal change history.

04 / Funding

No card referral commissions. Future contextual ads or donations must be disclosed and must not influence listings.

Privacy pledge

Filtering and comparisons run in your browser memory; we do not use analytics, cookies or third-party advertising scripts in this MVP. Important notices

This website is educational, not personalized financial, credit, legal or tax advice. Card terms change, and issuer agreements govern. Eligibility and approval decisions belong to issuers. Unreviewed records display placeholders. Even after review, the issuer agreement governs fees, APRs and rewards. No application or affiliate links are included. Always verify real card information directly with the issuer before acting.
